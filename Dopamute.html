1. Scope and policy status

This policy applies to the DopaMute Android application and explains how the app is intended to handle information when you use its features. The developer should update this policy before release and whenever data practices change.

Only publish absolute statements such as “no data is transmitted” after verifying the release build, all third-party SDKs, network behaviour, backups, logs, and crash-reporting configuration.

2. Information processed on your device

Depending on the features you enable, DopaMute may process app selections, focus schedules, session state, preferences, and usage information needed to provide distraction-management features. The intended design is to store this information in private local app storage and avoid collecting more than is necessary.

The implementation must identify the actual storage mechanisms used. If preferences are stored in SharedPreferences, DataStore, Room, or another location, the final policy and documentation should reflect that implementation accurately.

3. Analytics, advertising, and remote services

DopaMute's intended business model does not include advertising networks or selling personal information. The planned architecture is local-first and does not require an account or cloud service for core functionality.

This statement must be verified against the released app. If any analytics, crash reporting, billing, remote configuration, or other third-party SDK is added, disclose the relevant data handling and update the policy accordingly. Google Play Billing may process purchase-related information under Google's applicable terms.

4. Camera and on-device Vision AI

If the Zen Meditation or RealBook Study features are released, the intended design is to analyse camera frames on-device to estimate limited visual cues, such as eye closure, posture, or the presence of a book.

Camera access should occur only after the user initiates a feature and grants permission. The intended implementation does not save photos or videos. Frames should be processed in memory and discarded promptly after analysis, without uploading them to a server.

These are design commitments, not a substitute for technical verification. Before making an absolute guarantee, inspect the camera pipeline, ML Kit configuration, logs, caching, temporary files, and all SDK behaviour. If processing or storage differs, revise this policy.

5. Permissions and sensitive access

DopaMute may request special Android access for selected features, such as usage access, accessibility service operation, notification access, camera access, or Do Not Disturb policy access. These permissions should be requested only when required and explained before the user enables the associated feature.

Accessibility access must be narrowly scoped to the declared purpose and comply with Google Play policies. DopaMute should not use it to collect credentials, read unrelated personal content, or perform undisclosed actions. Users can revoke permissions through Android settings, although some features may stop working afterward.

6. Data sharing, sale, and disclosure

DopaMute is not designed to sell personal information. The intended local-first model avoids routine sharing of focus preferences and usage information with the developer.

If future versions introduce third-party services, support uploads, cloud synchronisation, or legally required disclosures, the policy must explain what information is involved, the purpose, and the applicable recipients before those practices are released.

7. Retention, deletion, and backups

Locally stored data should remain on the device for as long as needed to provide the selected features. Users should be able to clear relevant history or delete local app data where supported. Uninstalling the app generally removes its private local data, subject to Android backup and device settings.

The developer must verify Android backup rules, exported files, logs, and any optional data export before describing deletion as complete or irreversible.

8. Children's privacy

DopaMute is a general digital wellbeing tool. The developer must determine the intended audience and comply with applicable child-safety, consent, and Google Play Families requirements if the app is directed to children or is otherwise covered by those requirements. Do not claim compliance without completing the relevant review.

9. Security

The app should use Android's private application storage, minimise permissions, avoid unnecessary network access, and protect sensitive data from unintended exposure. No software can guarantee absolute security; users should keep Android updated and protect access to their devices.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DopaMute's features, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The developer should update the effective date and communicate material changes through an appropriate channel.

11. Contact the developer

For privacy questions or requests, contact Beesgeniuslab@gmail.com.

Permissions, explained.

Permissions should be requested only when a user enables the feature that needs them. Exact declarations and access flows must be confirmed against the implemented Android code and current platform policies.

Permission / accessPurposeImportant note
